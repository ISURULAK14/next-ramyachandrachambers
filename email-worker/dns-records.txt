--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECURITY EXPLANATION OF RECORDS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MX route1/2/3.mx.cloudflare.net:
   Routes all incoming mail directed to @ramyachandrachambers.com into the
   Cloudflare edge network, where our Email Routing Worker intercepts and processes it.

2. SPF include:_spf.mx.cloudflare.net include:spf.brevo.com include:_spf.resend.com ~all:
   Authorizes Cloudflare edge (for inbound forward rewrite), Brevo SMTP servers,
   and Resend SMTP servers to send email on behalf of @ramyachandrachambers.com.
   Softfail (~all) prevents rejection during transition while guaranteeing deliverability.

3. DMARC p=quarantine; pct=100;:
   Tells recipient servers (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo) to protect your domain against
   phishing and spoofing. Unauthorized emails claiming to be from @ramyachandrachambers.com
   are quarantined into spam, while reports are delivered directly to your SLT mailbox.
